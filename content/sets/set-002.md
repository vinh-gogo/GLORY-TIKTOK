## 🎙️ Luyện tập phản xạ Speaking (Part 2 - Describe a Picture)

### Hướng dẫn dàn bài 30 giây:
1. **Câu mở đầu (General view):** *This picture was taken on a busy street during the daytime.*
2. **Chi tiết chính (Foreground & Main subject):** *In the foreground, a few **pedestrians** are crossing the street using the **crosswalk**.*
3. **Chi tiết phụ (Background & Surrounding):** *On the right-hand side, some people are sitting comfortably at an **outdoor cafe** which **overlooks** the street.*
4. **Kết luận/Cảm nhận (Overall impression):** *Overall, the atmosphere appears lively and vibrant.*
